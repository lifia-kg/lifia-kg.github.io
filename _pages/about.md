The LIFIA Knowledge-Graph Team is a research group based at the National University of La Plata (UNLP), composed of members of the Laboratorio de Investigación y Formación en Informática Avanzada (LIFIA)(Laboratory for Research and Training in Advanced Informatics). The team includes researchers —both faculty members and graduates— as well as students from the institution.

Its work focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of graph-based data structures, particularly knowledge graphs, which enable the representation of complex problems within computational frameworks.

Currently, the team is developing the Land Value Observatory of the Province of Buenos Aires (OVS), a project aimed at assessing land value appreciation across the province. The observatory provides georeferenced visualization of properties for sale and serves as a data-driven support tool for territorial analysis.

To power this platform, the team employs, among other technologies, a knowledge graph based on the RealEstate ontology, which has been extended and refined by domain experts.
