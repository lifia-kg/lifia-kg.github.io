Diego Torres was born in La Plata and has built a remarkable career combining research, teaching, and creativity. He’s a researcher at LIFIA, Full Professor at UNLP, Associate Professor at UNQ, and Associate Researcher at CIC. Throughout his career, he has guided numerous students and research teams, always fostering innovation and collaboration. Beyond academia, he has a strong passion for music and writing, which reflect his creative and reflective side. He currently serves as the project director, bringing vision and leadership to the entire team.
