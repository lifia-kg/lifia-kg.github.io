Our scraper is a lightweight, modular tool designed to automatically collect real-estate classified listings for properties on sale across the Province of Buenos Aires. It extracts structured information from online listing platforms—including locations, prices, property features, and textual descriptions—and formats the data for integration into the OVS pipeline.
The scraper ensures consistent, up-to-date data acquisition and serves as the first step in constructing and enriching the real-estate knowledge graph.
